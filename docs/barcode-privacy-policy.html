Privacy Policy — QR Barcode Scanner & Generator
Effective date: October 6, 2026

Corebits Software Solutions Pvt Ltd provides QR Barcode Scanner & Generator for Android. Contact: support@corebitss.com. Website: https://corebitss.com.

Camera, photos and code content
Camera access is used to read codes. You can choose an image through Android's photo picker instead. Barcode recognition and code generation happen on your device. This app does not upload camera frames, selected images, decoded content, history, saved codes, folders or notes to a Corebits server. Android's permission controls let you revoke camera access.

Local storage and your choices
History, saved codes, favorites, folders, notes and batch quantities are stored in the app's local database. This Android app disables automatic app-data backup and device transfer. You can delete entries using selection in History or Saved. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it removes the local database. Exported files or shared images already saved outside the app are not removed by those actions.

Advertising and consent
The app displays Google AdMob banner and occasional interstitial (full-screen) ads and uses Google's User Messaging Platform for consent where applicable. Google's advertising SDK may collect and share information including IP address (which can indicate approximate location), ad and app interactions, diagnostic information, and device or other identifiers, for advertising, measurement, analytics and fraud prevention. Transfers use encryption in transit. Data handling depends on your consent choices, region, device settings and Google's services. Where required, the app's Scanner settings provides Privacy options to revisit advertising choices. Android also provides advertising identifier controls. See https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy/play-data-disclosure.

Barcode SDK
The app uses Google ML Kit for on-device barcode recognition. Input images and recognition results are processed on-device. The SDK can contact Google and collect device and app information, an installation identifier, usage and performance metrics, and diagnostic information to maintain and improve its services. See https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/terms and https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/android-data-disclosure.

Sharing and external actions
When you explicitly share a code image or CSV file, the recipient app receives the selected content. Opening a scanned link or choosing actions for email, telephone, messages, maps, contacts, calendar or Wi-Fi passes the relevant content to Android or another service. Those services operate under their own privacy terms. The app does not request access to your entire photo library, contacts, calendar or precise device location.

Accounts, retention and deletion
No account registration is required and Corebits does not operate a backend for scan content in this app. Local data remains until you delete it, clear storage or uninstall. Google determines retention for data handled by its SDKs under its policies; this app does not set that retention. Contact support@corebitss.com with privacy questions. Do not email sensitive scanned content unless it is necessary for your request.

Children
This app is a general-purpose code utility and is not designed specifically for children.

Changes
We may update this policy as the app or its data practices change. The effective date identifies this version.
