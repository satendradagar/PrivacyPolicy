4K Video Player All Formats · Corebits Software

Effective date and last updated: 26 October 2026

Corebits Software provides 4K Video Player All Formats (the “app”). This policy explains how information is handled when you use local playback, media management, browsing, downloads, status saving, casting, advertising and related features. For privacy questions or requests, contact support@corebitss.com.

1. Overview

  • The app does not require a Corebits Software account.
  • Your local media library is processed on your device after you grant permission or choose files or folders. Local playback and library scanning do not upload your media files to a Corebits Software server.
  • Online features connect to content providers and websites. Sharing and casting send information to the destinations you choose.
  • The app contains advertising. Advertising SDKs can collect and share device, interaction and diagnostic information. Optional analytics can process app-usage information when enabled in a configured release.

2. Local media and app information

With Android permission or file/folder access, the app reads media files and information such as filenames, folder locations, media URIs, duration, file size, audio/video tracks, subtitles and available artwork. This supports playback, search, thumbnails, playlists, resume positions and media management.

Playback history, favorites, bookmarks, playlists, appearance settings, local profile preferences and saved-folder references are stored locally. Features that rename, export, save or share files operate when you choose the relevant action.

The app does not request access to your contacts or precise device location. Advertising providers may infer a general location from your IP address. Android manages any biometric authentication; the app does not receive your fingerprint or face template. In-app hidden-media/PIN controls are not a guarantee that the underlying files are encrypted or inaccessible through other apps.

3. WhatsApp status saving

Status Saver reads the folder you approve through Android’s folder picker, normally Android > media > com.whatsapp > WhatsApp > Media > .Statuses. It displays photo/video statuses already available in that folder. It does not retrieve private accounts, remote messages or expired statuses from WhatsApp servers.

Saving creates a separate copy in a location selected by you or in the app’s Statuses media album. The app keeps a local index of saved copies and approved folder references. Original status files are not moved or deleted by the save action. Obtain the owner’s permission before saving or sharing their content.

4. Browser, online links and downloads

When you browse websites, search online, open streams, import remote playlists, use Discovery or check a download link, the app connects to the relevant providers. These can include sites you enter and discovery sources such as Internet Archive or Google/YouTube. Providers receive your IP address, requested URLs, search terms or content identifiers, and normal request information such as device/browser details.

The built-in browser can store cookies, cache, browsing history, bookmarks and website storage on your device. Websites can use their own cookies, sign-in systems and tracking technologies under their own policies. Where a supported download request uses an existing browser session, relevant cookies or request headers may be sent to that content provider. Link resolution may contact the provider and update its extraction components over the network.

Corebits Software does not operate a media-proxy service for these features. Downloads and their local records remain on your device. A publicly viewable link is not a guarantee of download availability or permission to save its content. Private, expired and DRM-protected content is not supported.

5. Advertising and consent

We use Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). Meta Audience Network or other advertising partners may participate when enabled through mediation. These services may process IP addresses, approximate location, advertising or app-set identifiers, device/app information, ad interactions and diagnostics to provide and measure advertising, protect against fraud and maintain their services.

The app uses Google’s User Messaging Platform to obtain and manage advertising consent where required. An advertising privacy-options control is available in Settings when the consent platform requires it. Availability of personalized, non-personalized or limited ads depends on your choices, region and the provider’s configuration. Non-personalized advertising can still involve identifiers or storage for measurement, security or frequency control.

You can also manage your advertising ID and ad-related choices through Android or Google settings, where available. Advertising partners handle information under their own policies, including Google’s Privacy Policy, Google’s advertising information and Meta’s Privacy Policy.

6. Analytics and casting

The app includes an optional Google Analytics for Firebase integration. When enabled in a configured release, it may process app interactions, app-instance/device identifiers, device information and advertising-impression information. Our custom analytics events do not include local media filenames, titles, file paths or media URLs. Advertising consent choices should not be assumed to disable all analytics or necessary service diagnostics. See Firebase privacy information.

When you choose Cast, compatible media links and related playback information are sent to the selected receiver and relevant media hosts. Google’s Cast SDK also processes technical and interaction information about discovery, sessions and casting to operate and improve the service. See Google Cast’s data disclosure.

If you choose voice search, Android’s selected speech-recognition service may process your audio under that service’s privacy terms. If you choose Share or open another app, the destination you select receives the content you send and applies its own policies.

7. Permissions

  • Media access and the Android file/folder picker: find and play selected media, display thumbnails, and manage approved files and folders. On older Android versions, storage permission may be used to save or modify files.
  • Notifications, foreground services and wake locks: support user-initiated background playback, downloads, progress and media controls.
  • Internet and network state: support online playback, browsing, discovery, downloads, ads and enabled service integrations.
  • Biometric authentication: support device-managed authentication for applicable privacy features.

You can review or revoke permissions in Android Settings. Revoking access may make the related feature unavailable without preventing use of unrelated features.

8. Retention and deletion

Local preferences and app records are generally retained until you remove them, clear the app’s storage or uninstall the app. Settings provides a playback-history clearing action. The browser’s Clear browsing data action removes its history, cookies, cache and website storage; saved bookmarks can be managed separately.

Downloaded media, exported files and status copies in shared storage or a location you selected may remain after uninstalling or clearing the app. Delete those files separately using the app’s available file controls or Android’s file manager. An index entry is not a second copy of the media itself.

Advertising, analytics and other providers determine retention for information they process under their policies and applicable configurations. Support correspondence is retained as needed to respond to your request, handle follow-up and meet applicable obligations. We cannot promise deletion of data controlled independently by third parties.

9. Security and international processing

The app uses Android’s permission and storage mechanisms. Google Mobile Ads documents encryption in transit for its SDK data. Security of websites, sharing destinations and receiving devices depends on those services and your choices. No storage or transmission system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Third-party services and email providers may process information in countries other than where you live. Their privacy policies describe their processing practices and applicable transfer protections.

10. Your choices and privacy requests

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of personal information, or to object to processing or withdraw consent. To make a request concerning information controlled by Corebits Software, email support@corebitss.com. Provide enough information to identify your request, but do not send passwords, private media or identity documents unless we explain why they are needed.

We may need to verify a request and may be limited by legal requirements or by not holding the information you seek. For files stored only on your device, use the local deletion controls described above. Requests about third-party accounts or information may also need to be directed to the relevant provider. You may contact your local data-protection authority where applicable.

11. Children and third-party content

The app is a general-purpose media utility and is not designed specifically for children. Parents and guardians should supervise access to websites and media. If you believe a child has provided personal information to Corebits Software, contact us so we can review the situation and take appropriate action.

The app is independent of WhatsApp, Instagram, YouTube and other content platforms. This policy does not replace the privacy policies or content terms of websites, advertising services or other apps you use through it.

12. Updates and contact

We may update this policy as the app or its data practices change. The date above identifies the latest revision. Material changes will be communicated where required.
