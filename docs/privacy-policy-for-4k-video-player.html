Privacy Policy for 4K Video Player All Formats

Effective date: [26-10-2026]

4K Video Player All Formats reads video and audio metadata on your device only after you grant Android media permissions. This access is used to display and play your local media library. Media content is not uploaded by 4K Video Player All Formats.

The app displays advertising through Google Mobile Ads and may use Meta Audience Network through AdMob mediation. Google, Meta, and participating advertising partners may process device identifiers, diagnostics, approximate location inferred from IP address, and interaction data to deliver, measure, and prevent fraud in advertising, subject to your consent choices and their policies. The app uses Google's User Messaging Platform to request required consent before loading ads and provides a Privacy choices control when required. The publisher must configure all production advertising partners and applicable messages in AdMob Privacy & messaging.

When configured by the publisher, Google Analytics for Firebase measures app interactions such as media-type selections, player opens, Cast starts, and advertising revenue. The app does not include media filenames, file paths, media URLs, or titles in these custom analytics events. Analytics collection is subject to the consent and configuration selected by the publisher and user.

When you explicitly open an online media URL or import a remote playlist, the app connects to the host you supplied to retrieve that media or playlist. That host can receive standard network information such as your IP address and request metadata. The publisher does not proxy or store this traffic.

We do not operate user accounts or intentionally collect names, email addresses, contacts, or precise location through the app. Android permissions can be changed at any time in system Settings. Ad-related choices may be managed through the consent message and your device or Google settings.
